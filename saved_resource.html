      Description
      NASA's Opportunity rover—a twin of Spirit—studied the history of climate and water at sites on Mars where conditions may once have been favorable to life.
      NASA's Opportunity rover was one of the most successful and enduring interplanetary missions. Opportunity operated almost 15 years, setting several records and making a number of key discoveries.
